# R1 conformance programs (owner GATES)

Generated by `scripts/seed/gen-r1-tests.py` (edit that file, rerun it, then `scripts/seed/oracle.sh r1`). Gate: `scripts/seed/gr.sh r1`
(positives equal to the stage-0 oracle, negatives refused) and the refusal-text golden `scripts/seed/g3.sh --rung r1 --update`
(run once, after R1 lands and the texts are reviewed; until then `refusal-r1.txt` does not exist).

* `feat/` 49 programs, one R1 feature each: do (4), when (4), cond (6), case (9), `->`/`->>` (5), dotimes (5), keywords as values (2),
  flat records (10), doseq (4). Results are recorded from stage-0 in `r1.oracle` (value, `trap`, or `refused`).
* `conf/` 8 typed adaptations of `lang/conformance` control/, records/, values/ programs (the originals are untyped Clojure-style
  `(defn main [])` programs outside the seed's typed `ns` + `:export` form; the originals are verbatim in `seed/tests/conformance/`
  and are refused by the seed at every rung until R5). Each adaptation keeps the original expected total where the R1 forms
  can express it (51, 50, 7, 21, 26, 16, 10, 7).
* `neg/` 40 negatives. `r1-neg.oracle` says whether stage-0 refuses the program too (`refused`, 30) or accepts it (`ACCEPTED`, 10:
  condp, if-not, when-not, assert, match, map literal, set literal, cond without :else, case without default, defprotocol). The
  ACCEPTED ones are refused by the seed because its subset is narrower; the R1 owner may accept one deliberately by listing it in
  `r1.accept` (n11 cond without :else and n24 case without default are the likely candidates).
* `r1.spec`: expectations for the 5 programs stage-0 REFUSES on native code (all doseq: stage-0's native lowering rejects `doseq`
  with an "if test is :i64" message), derived by hand, not measured.
* `r1.optional`: positives whose acceptance is an open decision (`map->Pt` needs map literals in the reader).
