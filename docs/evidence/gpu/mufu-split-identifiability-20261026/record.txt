THE MODEL (the twenty-eighth amendment's own frame, unchanged). A dependent
chain's cost is a sum of edge prices along the chain path. Variables: e_{i,j}
= price of the edge from an op of type i to a consumer of type j (self edges
included; M = MUFU.RSQ-class, F = FADD-class, U = any third same-accumulator
op), a_i = entry edge (seed producer -> first op) where the timed region
includes the chain's start, b_i = exit edge (last op -> sink consumer), f =
floor intercept. Every measurable quantity of every chain form is a PATH SUM
over these variables. Loop-carried bodies are cyclic path sums (consecutive
edges plus the loop back edge).

THE FINDING (design fact; REASONED and MECHANICALLY VERIFIED; NOT a
measurement):
(1) THE SPLIT IS UNIDENTIFIABLE IN EVERY FORM WHERE THE ENTRY EDGE SITS
    INSIDE THE TIMED REGION -- not just loop-carried bodies. The freedom is
    a potential gauge: for any per-node-type potential p, shifting e_{i,j}
    by p(j)-p(i) and the entry/exit edges correspondingly leaves EVERY path
    sum invariant. Open chains (the twenty-eighth amendment's proposed
    carrier) do NOT break it: they merely re-seat the freedom onto the
    entry/exit edges (families B above). Only path sums are observable; the
    only cross-pipe quantity any of these forms returns is the cycle sum
    e_MF + e_FM = 21.0000 (the measured pair slope, standing unchanged).
(2) THE TWENTY-EIGHTH AMENDMENT'S FORWARD PATH IS CORRECTED: an open-chain
    form is needed AND SUFFICIENT only under the entry-excluding timing
    discipline -- the first chain operand must be READY BEFORE the timing
    boundary so the entry edge is outside the measured functional, and the
    sink consumer must be common to all arms. Under that discipline the
    gauge collapses to a single sink-vs-floor trade that moves no edge
    price, and the split becomes identifiable with the estimators
        e_MF = V(MF) - V(F),   e_FM = V(FM) - V(M),
    where V(w) is the timed open-chain total of word w.
(3) FALSIFIABLE CLOSURE for that future instrument (pre-registered now, for
    whatever tick builds it): V(MFM) == V(MF) + V(FM) - V(F) and
    V(FMF) == V(FM) + V(MF) - V(M). If the closures fail, the discipline's
    assumption (entry edge outside the timed region) is wrong and the split
    stays unmeasured -- it must NOT be reported.
(4) The gauge also explains a standing loose end: the twenty-eighth
    amendment's per-form intercept differences (0.5234 / 7.4844 / 5.4805)
    live exactly in the floor-vs-entry freedom this analysis exhibits
    (family B's third null direction) and cannot be resolved from any
    intercept either.
