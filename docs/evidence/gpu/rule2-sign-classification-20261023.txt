  REGIME-SCOPED -- anything else. Cannot choose a variant.

TWO BOUNDARY CASES THE PROSE DID NOT DECIDE -- decided mechanically and
published, not hidden (the GAP-1.15 ethos applied to signs):

  1. The straddle's shape. Rule 2 named only "a sign that follows the
     regime" as regime-scoped. The committed corpus contains a straddle
     that does NOT follow the regime -- wsweep-low W = 32 :max, whose
     fast regime runs 4/6 are 0.958/1.017 (both sides inside ONE
     regime). The classifier therefore publishes the shape:
       by-regime     -- every regime one-sided, regimes disagree (sign
                        follows the regime; the W = 32 pattern);
       within-regime -- one regime holds both sides;
       no-side       -- every ratio exactly 1.0: no sign exists to claim.
     The shape NEVER changes the verdict: rule 2's second sentence
     already denies a regime-scoped sign any selection role whatever its
     shape.
  2. A ratio at exactly 1.0 sits on NEITHER side of 1.0, so a cell
     holding one is not regime-invariant ("the same side of 1.0" is read
     strictly).

The cell row is an admissibility roll-up over pooled per-run ratios only

asserted (rule 1's own named limit), so no cross-operator regime
