THE RULE (GAP-1.15), and where 1.15 comes from:

  Per operator, the per-run baseline median gpu-ns values sorted
  ascending, single-linkage: the next value joins the current level at
  <= 1.15x the previous value, else it starts a new level. Levels are
  published as gpu-ns ranges with run membership.

  The threshold is the geometric midpoint of the separation window the
  committed corpus establishes between prose-decided within-level drift
  and prose-decided between-level steps. The two anchors are exact
  committed numbers, not rounded estimates:

  - within-level, decided by prose: 38958 / 36229 = 1.0753 -- the
    wsweep-low-20261023/w32 :max cell's runs 4 and 6 are one "fast
    regime" in that record's NOTES (":max runs 4/6 0.958/1.017",
    named as fast-regime runs). Nothing manually placed in one level in
    the corpus is further apart.
  - between-level, decided by prose: 118646 / 96125 = 1.2343 -- the
    shape2-resweep-20261023/w512 :min cell's run 3 (96125) is
    "dropped" away from the slow level (118646..119312) in that
    record's NOTES. Nothing manually placed in two levels is closer.

  sqrt(1.0753 * 1.2343) = 1.1519... -> GAP_MAX = 1.15 (rounded down to
  two decimals; the boundary direction is documented: <= 1.15 merges).
