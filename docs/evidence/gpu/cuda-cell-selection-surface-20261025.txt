Part 1 -- ADOPTED: YES, a published cell verdict may enter the
selection surface -- under the entry contract below.

(1) UNIQUE-WINNER RULE. The record shape keys (device class,
    channel, W) -- see (3) -- so entry is decided per (W, shape)
    GROUP of cells, across pair types. At a group, arm v is the
    winner iff for every other arm u the (v, u) pair cell at that
    group is REGIME-INVARIANT with side "v faster" (rule 2 at the
    cell unit's letter, over the cell's pooled per-run ratios).
    Anything else REFUSES and the group keeps the design ladder's
    answer with no record: a scoped/straddled pair among the
    winner-candidates, a cycle, or no arm beating every rival. This
    is the measured-but-scoped refusal at its letter (ADR-0373's
    W = 32 low-band and shape-2 W = 512 refusals by citation: only
    a regime-robust verdict that names an answer may name one).

(2) BOTH CLOCKS OR REFUSE. The winner must hold identically in the
    gpu_ns and the host_ns cells of the group; a one-clock winner
    REFUSES. NAMED AS NEW: the ADR-0373 flip records ran one
    clock; this channel's instrument prints two, and the answer is
    metric-agnostic (the record carries no metric axis), so a flip
    cannot rest on one clock while the other refuses or
    contradicts. Rule 3's cross-clock magnitude ban is untouched --
    nothing is compared across clocks; both clocks must merely
    agree on the sign.

(3) RECORD SHAPE UNCHANGED (the ADR-0373 :measured-cell record's
    own shape). :device = the tested device class (xavier sm_72
    here), keyed the uB5G way (device-name-carries? reads
    :device/:name/:vendor of the descriptor -- an unnamed
    descriptor is not the tested class); :channel = the CUDA
    paired-timing channel; :route :cuda-v1 (either spelling);
    :workgroup-size = the group's W; :operators #{:sum :max :min}
    as a whole (the unit row's pooling by citation, per-operator
    entry stays rejected); :shapes = only the shapes whose own
    group qualifies; :evidence = the group's own session's runs,
    plus sanctioned cross-day replication runs when any exist --
    a same-day second session is flip-note PROSE ONLY (ADR-0373's
    same-day naming rule by citation).

(4) FLIP AND CONFIRM ARE BOTH ENTRY. Where the winner equals the
    design ladder's answer, the record CONFIRMS: the answer text
    does not change, :measured? becomes true and carries the
    record (the precedent already attaches :measured? true to a
    measurement-backed unchanged answer -- the kept-:baseline
    records whose :evidence names the refuting :subgroup-reduce
    runs). Where the winner differs, the record FLIPS the cell
    (the flip contract's role the unit row named). A refused group
    keeps :measured? false and no record.

(5) SCOPE. Entry is to select-variant's ANSWER CELLS only. Sign
    claims stay CHANNEL-SCOPED and DEVICE-SCOPED (a CUDA-cell
    verdict never flips an MSL or WGSL cell; an xavier verdict
    never acts past the xavier name class); no workgroup row in
    kotoba.compiler.accelerator-targets answers or moves
    (ADR-0355's 2026-10-25 channel rule by citation).

Part 2 -- REJECTED (named, not hidden):

(a) SINGLE-PAIR ENTRY -- flipping on the one pair cell whose sign
    opposes the design rung. Rejected: the three-arm ladder would
    then answer a variant measured SLOWER than its own lower rung
    at that W -- the answer means "run this variant", so the
    winner must beat every rival. The standing hazard SHAPE is the
    (op :sum, W = 64) interaction: :subgroup-reduce beats
    :warp-shuffle-reduce in every :sum run there (8/8, both
    sessions) while its pair against :baseline is scoped and often
    slower -- a single-pair flip could answer :subgroup-reduce at a
    cell where :baseline is measured faster.

(b) METRIC-SPLIT ENTRY (a record per metric, or a one-clock flip).
    Rejected: the record keys carry no metric axis and the answer
    is metric-agnostic; see (2).

(c) REGIME-SCOPED VERDICTS as partial or provisional basis (e.g.
    "flip now, revise if the other clock disagrees"). Rejected:
    the precedent's refusal; fail closed to the design ladder.

(d) PER-OPERATOR ENTRY -- already rejected at the cell-unit row
    (2026-10-25); unchanged and restated by citation.
