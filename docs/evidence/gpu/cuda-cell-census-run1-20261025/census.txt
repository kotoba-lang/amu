## Cell census (claim protocol rule 2 at cells, CUDA paired-timing channel)

Published by scripts/gpu_regime_classify.py under the adopted cell unit (ADR-0361's 2026-10-25 cell amendment, the ADR-0373 flip-cell precedent's unit shape adopted by citation): a cell is (metric, W, pair-type, shape) -- the reduce shape selected as a whole, the operator pool :operators #{:sum :max :min} pooled (the ONLY cross-stratum roll-up this channel carries; operators only, no pooling across W, pair-type, metric, shape, device or channel). The cell's own rule-2 verdict is rule 2 at its letter applied to the cell's pooled per-run ratios (each pair block's paired-median ratio in the named metric at that (W, shape, pair-type), one per operator): REGIME-INVARIANT FASTER / SLOWER is the ONLY selection role a cell verdict may play (the MSL channel's flip-cell role -- a per-W measured-cell flip of the kind :measured-cell records key); anything else (any straddle) is regime-scoped and no basis, in either direction. Rule 1 at the cell: the GAP-1.15 clusters of the pooled reference-arm per-run medians (one per operator present) are the cell's observed regimes.

This publication is a FULL CENSUS: every (metric, W, pair-type, shape) cell of the record's scan grid is published, so no cell is cherry-picked out of it. Metrics classify separately and never mix (rule 3 unchanged), and rule 3's publication limit stands -- cross-clock magnitude claims (gpu_ns vs host_ns) are INADMISSIBLE (different spans) -- so every cell row keeps both clocks' raw per-block times beside the named metric's ratio. A cell verdict is published ONLY over the full operator pool (the shape is selected as a whole): a cell missing an operator carries its ratios and names the gap but publishes NO verdict. A cell verdict belongs to the session that measured it and stays CHANNEL-SCOPED and DEVICE-SCOPED (a CUDA-channel sign never flips an MSL/WGSL cell and never answers a workgroup row; ADR-0355's 2026-10-25 channel rule stands); a same-day second session is corroboration and licenses nothing the cell's own session did not (ADR-0373's same-day rule by citation). Rule 4 is unchanged -- this publication moves no :measured? flag and no workgroup row.

Full census: 72 cells published (72 with a rule-2 verdict over the full operator pool, 0 naming an incomplete operator pool).
