PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS (fixed here, before any run):

Arm statistic (named once, used everywhere below): for a run r and an
(op, W, S) arm, med(r, op, W, S) = the median of that run's per-sample
per_dispatch_ns values for that arm (25 SAMPLE lines; the S=8 arms have
no samples and are out of scope, fail-closed as in sessions 1-2).
Sign at a cell for run r: SIGN(r, op, W) = "32faster" if
med(r, op, W, 32) < med(r, op, W, 16), else "16faster-or-tie".

P1r (sign recurrence at the two RUN-SENSITIVE cells, the row question):
at (sum, 128) and (max, 128) -- the two cells session 2 named
RUN-SENSITIVE -- the six new runs' SIGN values are published per run.
CONSISTENT at a cell iff all six share one sign; SPLIT at a cell iff the
six do not. A SPLIT is the pre-registered reproduction of session 2's
run-sensitivity (its run-1 vs runs 2/3 reversal), with the split counts
published. A CONSISTENT result means the anomaly did NOT reproduce in
six same-day fresh processes; it is NOT called absent (one same-day
batch of six cannot speak to cross-day or to rare recurrence).

P2r (the dip, secondary): per-run med(r, op, 128, 16) gpu_ticks values
are published for op in {sum, max}. DIP-RUN at (op, 128) is PRE-DEFINED
as a run whose med gpu_ticks for the (op, 128, 16) arm sits at least
1,000 ticks BELOW the median of the six runs' medians at that arm (the
1,000-tick threshold is named here because session 2's observed dip was
~3-4k ticks, well clear of it). The classification is secondary; the
per-run values are published either way. The CAUSE of any dip is not
measured by this protocol and stays UNMEASURED if one appears.

P3r (forcing reality + correctness): probe gl_SubgroupSize equals the
forced size at every forced cell and 32 at default cells, probe
gl_NumSubgroups = W / probe_size (any deviation VOIDS that cell's
timing with the void named, never dropped silently); mismatch = 0 at
every measured cell of every run (bit-exact CPU integer reference).

P4r (floor still lifted): the pooled median bracket gpu_ticks at every
measured (op, W, S=16/32/default) cell of the six new runs is >= 248
ticks (session 2's pre-registered floor-lift condition). A cell below is
NAMED floor-not-lifted and its S comparison is void with that reason.
