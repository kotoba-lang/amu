## THE DECISION

The native Metal counter-sample channel (the "counters" channel) is
DESIGN-ADOPTED as the claim-protocol GPU-core-timestamp measurement
channel on the narrowed device's host. The Vulkan-provider timestamp
route (VK_QUERY_TYPE_TIMESTAMP through the ADR 0371 provider) stays
PROPOSED and DEFERRED to that track. With the counters channel
designed, the ADR-0360 part-2 REOPEN CONDITION's second leg ("a
GPU-core-timestamp measurement of the device") is now DEFINED and
REACHABLE on this host -- it does NOT fire from this row: a leg fires
on a measurement, and nothing is measured here (rule 4).

SECOND-LEG LETTER DECISION (part of this row): does a counters-channel
record of the fold arms on the narrowed device satisfy the second
leg's letter ("a GPU-core-timestamp measurement of the device")?
DECIDED YES, with named scope. Ground: ADR-0360 part 3's own
parenthetical -- the leg "waits on a timestamp-capable route (a
Vulkan-provider route with timestampValidBits > 0 or a native Metal
counter channel)" -- so the ADR's text already contemplates a
NON-Chrome route's GPU-core-timestamp measurement as what the leg
waits on; the leg's letter is device-keyed, not route-keyed. Scope
named with the decision: the counters channel answers for the
MSL-EMITTED arms of the same KIR reduce shape (the repository's own
MSL emitter, the arms the MSL flip campaign ran); the WGSL route's
arms are NOT covered (Chrome owns its command buffers and the WebGPU
timestamp routes are CLOSED, gpu-timestamp-route-probe-20261025), so
the WGSL channel's own per-kernel GPU time stays UNMEASURED until a
Vulkan-provider timestamp route exists. Whether the leg's evaluation
(the measurement row, its own later slice) reaches the narrowing's
route-scope question from an MSL-route counters record is named as the
evaluation row's question, NOT pre-answered here.

## THE CHANNEL CONTRACT

1. WHAT IT MEASURES. Per-dispatch GPU-core time of ONE reduce dispatch,
   in GPU nanoseconds, as the delta of two GPUTimestamp counter
   samples bracketing the dispatch inside the compute encoder
   (sampleCountersInBuffer:atSampleIndex:withBarrier: immediately
   before and after the dispatch; the samples are resolved with
   resolveCounters:inRange:destinationBuffer:destinationOffset: into a
   shared buffer and read back). The bracket is the design's
   substitute for the unavailable dispatch-boundary sampling point
   (probe fact: dispatch sampling 0) -- NAMED, not hidden: whether the
   sample-pair delta equals the dispatch's GPU time or carries
   sampling/barrier overhead is UNMEASURED and is the measurement
   row's own calibration question.
2. UNIT AND PROTOCOL. The claim protocol carries IN FULL (rules 1-4):
   rule 1's baseline metric gpu-ns is this channel's NATIVE metric --
   unlike the WGSL wall-clock channel's metric-named adoption, no
   metric renaming happens (the protocol was written on Metal GPU
   timestamps); GAP-1.15 clusters over per-run baseline medians, the
   MSL harness idiom's run unit (one fresh process per block, paired
   rounds, per-run median, the pair's paired-median ratio). Rule 2:
   regime-matched sign only, CHANNEL-SCOPED (a counters-channel sign
   flips no other channel's cell and answers no workgroup row). Rule
   3: within-channel regime-matched magnitudes; the counters channel
   is its own instrument and its magnitudes NEVER merge with either
   existing instrument's (the command-buffer GPUStartTime->GPUEndTime
   bracket -- the MSL campaign's gpu-ns source -- and any host
   wall-clock); NAMED: all three say "ns" and are still three
   instruments. Rule 4 unchanged.
3. INSTRUMENT DISTINCTION (why this row exists at all). The MSL
   campaign's gpu-ns is the COMMAND BUFFER's GPUStartTime->GPUEndTime
   around one reduce dispatch; it brackets the buffer's GPU execution,
   not the kernel. The kernel-time question (the arm-vs-arm speed
   answer the wall-clock read could not give -- common host/queue
   overhead biases wall-clock paired ratios toward 1 and can hide a
   real win) needs the per-dispatch counter samples. The two
   instruments stay separate under rule 3 (above).
4. SCOPE. The narrowed device class (uB5G-keyed "m1 max"), this host,
   the MSL-emitted fold arms (the reduce arms :subgroup-reduce vs
   :baseline of the same KIR shape the MSL flip campaign ran). No
   other device, route, arm family or workload is covered.

## REJECTED / DEFERRED, NAMED

(a) Vulkan timestamp queries (VK_QUERY_TYPE_TIMESTAMP) through the
    wire-42 runtime route -- DEFERRED, PROPOSED (neither implemented
    nor accepted): the loader's broker is gone (2026-10-22) and the
    ADR 0371 provider that replaces it is not implemented; the route
    stays the runtime-side successor (its own row when a provider
    answers). This record does not decide it.
(b) Chrome/Dawn writeTimestamp routes -- CLOSED by the 2026-10-25
    probe (measured route absence), unchanged here.
(c) WebGL2 EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2 -- REJECTED again: it
    measures the GL/ANGLE channel, a different execution path from
    the reduce arms (the probe's route-6 ruling, cited).
(d) Command-buffer GPUStartTime->GPUEndTime AS the kernel-time answer
    -- REJECTED: it is a command-buffer bracket, not a kernel bracket
    (instrument distinction, contract point 3); it remains the MSL
    campaign's valid metric source and is not deprecated.
(e) Measuring the WGSL route's arms through the counters channel --
    IMPOSSIBLE (Chrome owns its command buffers), named above; the
    WGSL route's kernel time is not this channel's to answer.
