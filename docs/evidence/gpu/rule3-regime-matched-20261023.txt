WHAT LANDED (implemented, with tests) -- scripts/gpu_regime_classify.py:

- median_draw(ratios): the regime mix of a paired median -- which runs
  draw it (the middle run(s) of the (ratio, run) sort). TIES ARE NAMED,
  NOT RESOLVED: every run tying a middle-position ratio is a candidate
  draw, so a tie cannot silently pick the pure stratum.
- draw_levels / strata_matched: draw purity (the draw lies within one
  rule-1 level) and cross-cell stratum matching by rule 1's OWN
  single-linkage test at GAP-1.15 (some member median of one level
  within 1.15x of some member median of the other). The harness reads
  no clock: "matched" is gpu-ns compatibility under the same constant
  that defines a level within a session -- NOT verified cross-session
  clock identity. The corpus prose's cross-session slow/fast naming is
  the assumption this replaces with a visible test.
- regime_matched_comparison / cell_regime_matched_comparison /
  render_rule3_section + CLI `--compare <historical.md> <new.md>`: the
  three-phrase reproduction vocabulary, mechanically. Sign phrase:
  "reproduces in sign strictly" exactly when the historical cell is
  rule-2 regime-invariant on one side and every new-cell per-run ratio
  holds it (a regime-scoped historical cell has no single historical
  sign to hold -- named). Magnitude phrase keyed on the regime mix of
  the two median draws: matched -> "magnitude reproduces within
  <delta>" (delta = the observed deviation of the PUBLISHED paired
  medians, quoted from the summary's own median column so the phrase
  digits match the committed prose); mixed -> "magnitude splits by clock
  regime", the deviation attributed to session mixing, NEVER "failed to
  reproduce". The comparison is ALSO drawn within every matched stratum
  pair (per-stratum ratio medians + delta), published beside the
  cell-median delta -- the regime-matched datum rule 3 asks for.
