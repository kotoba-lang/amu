THE FINDING (the occupancy branch is REFUTED at the measured
granularity; the parity is a latency-side MARGIN MINIMUM of the
subgroup arm):
  (1) OCCUPANCY IS NOT THE CAUSE -- it is IDENTICAL where the ratio
      moves. Every emitted kernel at W in {64,128,256,512,1024} sits
      at 100% warp occupancy in BOTH arms and ALL THREE ops
      (blocks_per_sm x W/32 = 64 of 64 resident warps; at W=256: 8
      blocks/SM x 8 warps = 64); W=32 is 50% for every arm and op
      alike (32 blocks x 1 warp, a launch-shape limit). Registers are
      flat across widths and nearly flat across ops: subgroup 16
      (sum) / 15 (max,min), baseline 10, warp-shuffle 14 at EVERY
      width; ptxas reports 0 bytes stack frame, 0 spill stores, 0
      spill loads for all 54 kernels. The only sum-specific resource
      anywhere is the subgroup sum kernel's ONE extra register -- which
      it carries at every width, including W=128/512 where the ratio
      is solidly below 1.0. Nothing in the census is W=256-specific.
  (2) THE PARITY IS A LATENCY-SIDE PHENOMENON (DERIVED -- a parse of
      the COMMITTED wide-pairs measure-log, no new hardware; 200
      samples per arm per cell, the 400 smoke orphans excluded by
      reading only the grid section): at W=256 the subgroup sum kernel
      is STILL FASTER than baseline sum in absolute median gpu_ns
      (120128 vs 120752 at n=1000003; 125360 vs 126048 at n=1048576)
      -- the ratio is 0.9948/0.9945, below 1.0, but the margin is only
      ~0.5%, versus 3.4-3.6% at W=128 (112544 vs 116544; 116688 vs
      120864) and 6.7-7.3% at W=512 (129936 vs 139712; 135488 vs
      146128). The W=256 sum cell is a MARGIN MINIMUM of the subgroup
      arm's advantage: a sub-1% edge sits inside the 20-pair noise --
      exactly the fifteenth amendment's weighted coin -- while the
      neighbour widths' 3-7% edges cannot flip.
  (3) THE MARGIN MINIMUM'S SHAPE IS THE TWO ARMS' PER-BLOCK COST
      CURVES (DERIVED): amortized gpu_ns per block (grid time /
      ceil(n/W)) for baseline sum steps 14.85 -> 30.85 -> 71.45
      (x2.08 then x2.32 over W = 128 -> 256 -> 512) while subgroup sum
      steps 14.3 -> 30.65 -> 66.35 (x2.14, x2.16 -- near-uniform).
      Baseline's serial shared-memory tree degrades disproportionately
      at W=512 (9 __syncthreads steps) and sits at its best relative
      point at W=256, where the subgroup arm's cross-subgroup fold
      (3 __syncthreads steps at nsg = 8) buys it almost nothing over
      the baseline tree's 8 steps.
  (4) THE OPERATOR STRADDLE LIVES IN THE SUBGROUP ARM ONLY: baseline's
      absolute medians are op-FLAT (sum/max/min within ~0.4% at every
      W, both shapes: e.g. W=256 n=1000003 121360/121248/120752)
      while subgroup's split into sum vs max/min (~12-13% at every
      W). The quartet's max/min members sit solidly above 1.0 not
      because max/min kernels are slow in absolute terms but because
      the subgroup arm's max/min variants pay ~12-13% over its own sum
      variant at every width.
