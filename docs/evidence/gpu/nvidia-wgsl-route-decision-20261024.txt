THE DECISION (ADR-0361's 2026-10-24 amendment, decided in three parts;
the full text lives in the ADR):

YES -- the WGSL route (:webgpu / :wgsl-v1, one route two names, the
ADR-0359 decision-3 idiom) carries NVIDIA's :subgroup-reduce row under
the SAME rules 3 + 6 gate as the Vulkan route.

  Part 1 -- the row extends, with NO ladder on this route:
    :warp-shuffle-reduce is CUDA emission only (rule 3: admission first),
    so the row is the route's first and only variant rung --
    :subgroup-reduce -> :baseline, total and deterministic. The CUDA
    ladder's precedence question does not arise here.
  Part 2 -- the sm_70+ floor rides along, unchanged: it is NVIDIA's
    row-wide requirement (a REPORTED capability below 7.0, or a malformed
    one, fails closed to :baseline; an absent capability is not a
    refusal), and the table's gate is route-agnostic, so it binds this
    rung exactly as it binds the Vulkan and CUDA rungs. No new fact, key
    or floor is invented.
  Part 3 -- the rationale is the AMD row's plus route reality: the WGSL
    arm is the same runtime-width fold (subgroup_size queried at
    runtime), so the AMD row's heterogeneity answer ("the RDNA
    wave32/wave64 duality is answered by the emission's runtime width
    query and rule 6's S | W gate, not by selection") answers NVIDIA's
    fixed warp width a fortiori -- 32 is just the reported S. And
    WebGPU-on-NVIDIA is a deployed surface; a dispatch there previously
    answered :baseline even where the device reports the fold's facts.
