THE DECISION (three parts; ACCEPTED DESIGN ONLY -- this is a design
decision, nothing measured here):

YES -- a WGSL measurement under the claim protocol exists, by the
protocol's own adoption clause ("a later vendor campaign adopts it by
citation, its own row" -- ADR-0360, claim-protocol amendment; this
slice IS that row). The adoption is METRIC-NAMED and SIGN-SCOPED,
because the adopting channel cannot produce the metric the protocol was
written on.

  Part 1 -- THE ADOPTION CONTRACT (what makes a WGSL record
  claim-protocol):

  (a) Rule 1 carries with the baseline metric NAMED BY THE CHANNEL.
      For the WGSL wall-clock channel the per-run baseline median is the
      channel's own per-run median host submit->completion time per
      operator (per cell-arm run), and the GAP-1.15 clusters are named
      in that metric's units (host-ms ranges, not gpu-ns ranges). The
      clustering rule is metric-parametric as written -- single-link
      over per-run medians (scripts/gpu_regime_classify.py's mechanism
      carries unchanged; THIS SLICE DOES NOT TOUCH that script) -- only
      the publication names the metric. A claim-protocol WGSL record
      publishes, under rule 1: the per-run baseline medians per
      operator in the named metric, and the regime clusters.

  (b) Rule 2 carries IN FULL, unchanged: a sign claim is
      regime-invariant or regime-scoped, and only a regime-invariant
      sign is a selection basis ("every per-run ratio of every observed
      regime lies on the same side of 1.0"). Sign claims are
      CHANNEL-SCOPED: a WGSL-channel sign never flips an MSL cell and
      never answers a workgroup row (the mirror of the standing rule
      "an MSL measurement never flips a WGSL cell"), and only a
      WGSL-route cell's own selection question can consume it.

  (c) Rule 3 carries with a NAMED LIMIT: magnitude claims stay
      within-channel and regime-matched as written; CROSS-METRIC
      magnitude claims (host-clock vs GPU-timestamp) are INADMISSIBLE.
      Named hazard (design reasoning, NOT a measurement): fixed
      host/queue overhead common to both arms biases paired ratios
      toward 1 -- conservative against win claims (it can hide a real
      win; it cannot invent one through the common part) -- while
      ASYMMETRIC arm overhead could bias either way and is not assumed
      away. Therefore every magnitude publication on this channel keeps
      both arms' raw per-round times beside the ratios (the WGSL harness
      already prints them; a record that hides them is inadmissible
      under rule 1's publication discipline).

  (d) Rule 4 carries unchanged: this adoption measures nothing and
      moves nothing.

  Part 2 -- WHAT THE ADOPTION DOES NOT DO:

  - The 2026-10-25 wall-clock read stays OUT. It published no rule-1
    classification (GROUNDING 3) and predates this adoption; its parity
    verdict and its maps-onto-no-row ruling are untouched. This
    decision does not retroactively promote it.
  - No workgroup row (ADR-0355/0358), :measured? flag, :shapes value or
    selection answer moves (rule 4). The dispatch-row channel rule
    (ADR-0355, "Amendment (2026-10-25, the measurement channel for row
    flips)") is untouched: a claim-protocol WGSL measurement is CLAIM
    currency for the narrowing's route scope and STILL maps onto no
    dispatch row. Two different words "channel" for two different
    things, kept apart on purpose: the row-flip channel (route /
    workload / pairing / clock / provenance -- what evidence may answer
    a workgroup row) and the claim-protocol channel (what evidence may
    carry a sign or magnitude claim).

  Part 3 -- CONSEQUENCE for ADR-0360 part 2's narrowing (whose REOPEN
  CONDITION this defines):

  The first leg -- "a claim-protocol WGSL measurement" -- is now
  DEFINED and is REACHABLE on this adapter's host-clock channel. No
  GPU-core timestamp is needed for the first leg. The second leg --
  "a GPU-core-timestamp measurement" -- stays REFUSED on this adapter
  (GROUNDING 2) and waits on a timestamp-capable route (the prior
  record's HONEST LEFT 1: a Vulkan-provider route with
  timestampValidBits > 0, or a native Metal counter channel --
  UNDESIGNED, its own row).

  Named missing pieces before the first leg can FIRE (each its own
  later slice):

    (i) harness-side rule-1 classification for the WGSL harness: a
        run's log/summary must publish the GAP-1.15 clusters on the
        WGSL baseline metric (the 2026-10-25 log format is one carrier
        candidate). UNIMPLEMENTED -- this slice moves no code.
    (ii) a fresh WGSL run of the narrowed device's two fold arms whose
        record satisfies rules 1 + 2. UNMEASURED -- no hardware ran in
        this slice.

  UNMEASURED, named so no reading upgrades: whether the WGSL
  host-clock baselines on this adapter cluster into more than one
  regime level at all. If the session is single-level, rule 2's
  regime-invariance degenerates to the protocol's own single-level
  standard -- every per-run ratio on one side of 1.0 -- and by THAT
  standard the 2026-10-25 read would have FAILED (its per-run ratios
  straddle 1.0 in both metrics; the log's own aggregate says subgroup
  faster in 13/36 single and 17/36 batch32 pairs). The sign of any
  future claim-protocol WGSL run is UNMEASURED. Per-kernel GPU time on
  this adapter stays UNMEASURED (refused). The narrowing itself is
  unchanged: parity is not a fold win, the conservative :baseline
  answer for the named device stands, no :measured-cell record moves.
