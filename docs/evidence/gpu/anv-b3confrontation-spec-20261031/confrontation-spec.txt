2. THE CONFRONTATION REQUIREMENT (this is the decision). Any future
   pre-registered narrowing claim at mid (n=254,882) or bis (n=255,045)
   satisfies "confronts this cut's 16-block content first" only if its
   OWN pre-registration -- frozen before any run of its own cut, with
   its own freeze stamp, the standing rule -- contains ALL four parts
   below. A claim that does not contain them is not a confrontation and
   is refused at review; this spec never interprets a missing part as
   satisfied.

   (a) CONTENT CONFRONTATION: the pre-registration QUOTES the claimed
   point's s139 16-block content (the 16 block classes, the block
   medians, the block 18-cell minima, the pooled-48 class -- section 1
   (i) above or its parsed source) and STATES which of its own letters
   supersedes which committed reading. Silence about this content is
   not confrontation.

   (b) BLOCK-GRANULARITY FLOOR: the claim's own cut runs AT LEAST 16
   disjoint blocks of 3 runs per B=3 arm (the frozen estimator unit) at
   byte-identical pinned binaries at the claimed point, with its
   stability letter pre-registered over the block classes in the s139
   C2/C3 shape (single-class across ALL blocks AND the pooled arm
   passes; mixed blocks fail). The s139 content at BOTH points is
   BLOCK-UNSTABLE (7x CAPTURED + 9x INTERMEDIATE/MIXED at each), so a
   claim AT either point cannot pass this floor on the committed
   content alone -- it needs its own fresh single-class-stable content
   at this granularity or finer. More blocks of the same mixed shape do
   not confront anything; they re-read it.

   (c) STATISTIC CONFRONTATION: because the frozen class rule rides
   CAPTURED-vs-not on the block's 18-cell MIN at the 1,000-tick
   threshold, and that statistic is measured within-window unstable at
   byte-identical binaries near the boundary (section 1 (ii)/(iii): the
   min crosses 1,000 inside ONE window at ONE binary, and the s139
   block minima straddle it at both points while all block medians sit
   1,127..1,174), a claim whose class discrimination at its points
   depends on the min statistic landing on one side of 1,000 must
   pre-register BEFORE RUNS exactly one of:
     (i) a different class statistic (with its own rationale and its own
         frozen thresholds -- e.g. a pooled-median band against frozen
         references); or
     (ii) a tolerance rule that confronts the MEASURED wander band
         (block-unit 981..1,015; per-run unit 974..1,023 -- descriptive
         unit) and pre-registers what its letters do when a block
         minimum lands inside that band (the s34 straddle -- 2 of 12
         blocks CAPTURED in one window -- is the named hazard); or
     (iii) points whose block minima are measured single-class stable
         OUTSIDE the band at its own >=16-block content (min >= 1,015+
         at every block, or <= 981- at every block).
   A statistic chosen after the runs are read is never a confrontation.

   (d) CLAIM-FORM CONFRONTATION: any narrowing claim that touches the
   bracket's disputed record QUOTES the dual-class content and the
   cross-window disagreement (section 1 (iv)) and says what its letters
   do with them. The MID-ONLY NARROWING CLAIM stays SUSPENDED until a
   claim passes (a)-(c) at mid and confronts (d); a claim that rests on
   one window at mid must name the other windows at the same pinned
   binary (s132/s136/s139) and pre-register its own cross-window
   letter.
