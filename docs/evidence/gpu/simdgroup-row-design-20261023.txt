WHAT THE DECISION SAYS (the full text is ADR-0373; the load-bearing
clauses):

- A new variant `:simdgroup-reduce` is DESIGNED: the reduce shape
  (:sum/:max/:min, W >= 32) on the MSL route ONLY, whose intra-SIMD-group
  fold is Metal's simd_sum/simd_max/simd_min, followed by the SAME
  cross-SIMD-group tree over the W/S partials (ADR-0357's P1/P2 bind the
  tree unchanged). A SEPARATE variant -- the negative pins that keep
  :subgroup-reduce's fold the shuffle loop stand unchanged.
- P1 binds by option (b): simd_sum's association order is out of the
  emitter's hands and is not pinned by the vendor documentation this
  decision cites (the spec text was NOT re-read this tick -- named, and
  the P1 scope does not depend on it); the :sum claim is scoped ("low
  bits only, for inputs without catastrophic cancellation").
- Selection, two clauses: (1) the measured-cell carve-out -- a device
  class whose reduce-shape answers are measured or decided keeps them;
  M1 Max keeps :baseline at every W, and :simdgroup-reduce does not
  answer there until a measurement of THIS variant flips that cell;
  (2) the MSL ladder (ADR-0361's CUDA-ladder idiom): outside the
  carve-out, :simdgroup-reduce is the vendor-native first rung behind the
  new rule-6 fact `:simd-reduce`, :subgroup-reduce the second rung,
  :baseline otherwise.
- Rule 3 gates the implementation (emission + lower admission + tests
  FIRST, then the selection predicate). Nothing is implemented by this
  slice.
