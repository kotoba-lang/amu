2. THE BAR (frozen; this is ACCEPTED DESIGN ONLY -- it is not code in
   the compiler and it is not a measurement):

   READING: one 3-run block -- 3 fresh processes pooled over 25
   rounds per cell, the frozen estimator unit of every cross-session
   reading in (F3) -- at the anchor knob triple (BATCH=3,
   WORK_MUL=1024, NDIV=4033, n=253,906 = (1,000,003*1024)//4033,
   computed programmatically) at a byte-identical rebuilt binary with
   the session's build replication pin verified, in a session where:
   (i) the frozen C1 archived-control replication fired branch (a)
   (both c2 and c8 satisfy their criteria); (ii) VERIFY mismatch is 0
   everywhere (the frozen INVALIDATION criterion of the measurement
   protocol, adopted here); (iii) the custody GO stamp precedes the
   builds and the runs. A reading carries its UTC day and its session
   evidence directory.

   COUNTED: the FIRST qualifying reading of each UTC day, in ledger
   order. Later qualifying same-UTC-day sessions are NAMED DESCRIPTIVE
   CONTENT and do NOT advance the bar -- the L4 axis (the UTC day) is
   the last unmeasured replication axis and the bar must span it;
   same-day multiplicity was shown by sessions 26-29 to flip class and
   is inside the named noise floor (F2). A FLAGGED session (see below)
   consumes NO day slot: the day's first later qualifying session may
   still count.

   FLAGGED (not counted; named in the ledger): C1 fired (b), or VERIFY
   mismatch > 0, or the build pin is not byte-identical, or the
   custody GO is missing/after the builds. The session is recorded and
   adds no reading.

   VERDICT over the first four COUNTED readings (the evaluator's
   procedure; order of checks is part of the freeze):
   (V1) CONTRADICTION -- any COUNTED reading classifies FLOOR, or any
        COUNTED median is out of band. Out of band := NOT within +/-5%
        of EACH frozen numeric reference (1,129 / 1,132 / 1,131 --
        session 29's IN-BAND definition, adopted here). Pre-named
        contradiction / out-of-band; measured content; the bar does
        not resolve it and does not discard the reading.
   (V2) UNSETTLED -- four COUNTED readings exist but their classes
        split (CAPTURED vs INTERMEDIATE/MIXED), or they are one class
        but their medians are not mutually within 5% (max/min - 1 >
        0.05). The class is UNSTABLE at the frozen threshold across
        UTC days as well; the threshold-artifact-vs-representation
        probe (the (2048,8066) twin -- the REPRESENTATION side that
        sessions 24/26's C3 (b) splits left open) takes priority. NO
        SILENT EXTENSION: a split at four does NOT extend to eight by
        default. Any extension is a NEW pre-registered bar, never a
        re-roll.
   (V3) SETTLED c -- four COUNTED readings from four distinct UTC
        days, all classifying the same class c (CAPTURED or
        INTERMEDIATE/MIXED), every median in band and mutually within
        5%. The anchor's class at the frozen threshold is SETTLED to
        c.
   (V4) PENDING n/4 -- fewer than four COUNTED readings. The bar
        reports progress and settles nothing.

   WHAT THE BAR SETTLES: ONLY the frozen class of the (1024,4033)
   anchor at n=253,906, B=3 at the frozen section-4 threshold (FLOOR
   <= 100 ticks at all 18 pipeline-ok cells; CAPTURED >= 1000 at all

   the bisect only through a NEW pre-registered cut session (whose

   COST (named in advance): at least four qualifying UTC days (one
