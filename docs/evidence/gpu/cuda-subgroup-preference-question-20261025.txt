PART 2 -- WHY NO UNDER THE STANDING PROTOCOL:

  (a) Rule 2 (the adoption's (b), carried at its letter): a
      regime-invariant sign is the ONLY selection basis. The
      (op :sum) warp/subgroup stratum is REGIME-SCOPED in both
      sessions, both metrics, and the straddle is WITHIN regime 1:
      rule 1's GAP-1.15 clustering pools the W = 64 runs with
      W = 128..512 (the reference arm :subgroup-reduce's per-run
      medians at W = 64 and W = 128 are 111248 / 111168 in run1,
      within the 1.15 window), so the W = 64 cell is NOT its own
      regime level and its one-sided sign is not a regime-invariant
      sign anywhere. The reproduced publication's own verdict text:
      "straddle within regime 1 (one regime holds both sides)".
      "A regime-scoped sign cannot choose a variant, in either
      direction" -- the selection-answer record's ruling stands.
  (b) Per-cell selection units do not exist on this channel. The
      adoption's rule-2 unit is the (metric x operator x pair-type)
      stratum and "names no roll-up across them". The Apple
      flip-cell precedent's "the cell's OWN claim-protocol rule-2
      verdict at that shape -- REGIME-INVARIANT FASTER" is a
      DIFFERENT unit this channel has never published. Adopting one
      is a rule-2 amendment -- its own design row, PROPOSED here
      and deliberately NOT decided in this row.
  (c) Scope limits unchanged: ONE NVIDIA device (xavier, L4T
      R35.4.1, sm_72); two agreeing sessions bind no distribution
