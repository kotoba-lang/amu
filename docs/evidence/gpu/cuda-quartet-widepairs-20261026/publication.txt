## Regime classification (claim protocol rule 1, CUDA paired-timing channel)

Published by scripts/gpu_regime_classify.py (GAP-1.15) over the paired-timing instrument log. This channel's baseline metric is NAMED BY THE CHANNEL (the 2026-10-25 claim-protocol CUDA adoption, ADR-0361): gpu_ns -- CUDA-event elapsed around the kernel on the GPU timeline -- with host_ns (CLOCK_MONOTONIC submit->completion) as the channel's second metric; the two NEVER mix in one clustering or one claim. A regime here is a cluster of the observed reference-arm per-run median levels in that metric, per (operator x pair-type) stratum -- the reference arm is the paired ratio's denominator (:baseline for subgroup/baseline and warp-shuffle/baseline, :subgroup-reduce for warp-shuffle/subgroup), so a cluster pools runs whose proxy is the same kernel.

Run unit on this channel (the adoption's "one fresh process per pair block"): one run is one pair block -- (n, W, operator, pair-type) -- the fresh process that sampled PAIRS=20 interleaved pairs (WARMUP=2 discarded per arm, alternating AB/BA order). The per-run baseline median is the pair block's reference-arm median over its 20 samples in the named metric.

Clustering rule GAP-1.15: per stratum, the per-run reference-arm medians sorted ascending, single-linkage -- the next median joins the current level at <= 1.15x the previous, else it starts a new level. The rule is metric-parametric as written; only the publication names the metric. Rule 2's sign classification is published in its own section beside this one (regime-matched sign per level over this record's pair blocks) -- this rule-1 section itself renders no sign verdict -- and rule 4 is unchanged: this publication moves no `:measured?` flag and no workgroup row.

## Sign classification (claim protocol rule 2, CUDA paired-timing channel)

Published by scripts/gpu_regime_classify.py from the record's own pair blocks (rule 2's unit on this channel: the per-run ratio is the block's paired-median ratio in the named metric -- median over the block's within-pair ratios armA/armB -- and rule 2's correctness precondition is verified inside the measurement, as the instrument does) and the rule-1 regime clusters of the same runs' reference-arm per-run medians (GAP-1.15). Rule 2: a sign is regime-invariant when every per-run ratio of every observed regime lies on the same side of 1.0 -- only such a sign may be a selection basis; any other sign is regime-scoped and cannot choose a variant. The sign machine is the MSL channel's, unchanged and metric-parametric; gpu_ns and host_ns are TWO metrics of this channel and classify separately, never mixed.

Sign claims on this channel are CHANNEL-SCOPED (the 2026-10-25 adoption carries rule 2 in full): a CUDA-channel sign never flips an MSL/WGSL cell and never answers a workgroup row (ADR-0355's 2026-10-25 channel rule stands: the instrument channel answers no dispatch row); only the CUDA-route selection rows consume it. Rule 4 is unchanged -- this publication moves no `:measured?` flag and no workgroup row. NO cross-stratum roll-up is published: the adoption defines strata at (metric x operator x pair-type) and names no roll-up across them (a roll-up would be its own design row).
