DECISION (the design slice; ADR-0361's new amendment is the normative
text -- this record grounds it):

  (a) Rule 1 carries with the channel-named baseline metric: the pair
      block's REFERENCE arm (the paired ratio's denominator --
      :baseline for subgroup/baseline and warp-shuffle/baseline,
      :subgroup-reduce for warp-shuffle/subgroup) median over its 20
      samples. Two metrics, never mixed: gpu_ns (rule 1's own metric
      class, carried at its letter here) and host_ns (secondary, its
      own clusters). Clusters per (metric x operator x pair-type),
      GAP-1.15 unchanged -- the reference arm differs by pair-type and
      the baseline is a clock-level proxy, so a cluster pools runs
      whose proxy is the same kernel. A claim in a metric whose
      clusters are unpublished is inadmissible.
  (b) Rule 2 carries IN FULL, unchanged: regime-invariant sign is the
      only selection basis; the per-run ratio is the block's
      paired-median ratio in the named metric; correctness
      precondition verified inside the measurement (the instrument's
      verified read-back: 218/218 ok=1 in the committed run). Sign
      claims CHANNEL-SCOPED: a CUDA-channel sign never flips an MSL or
      WGSL cell and never answers a workgroup row (ADR-0355's
      2026-10-25 channel rule: the instrument channel answers no
      dispatch row); only the CUDA route's own selection questions
      (HONEST LEFT (c)'s rows) consume it.
  (c) Rule 3 with NAMED LIMITS: magnitude claims within-channel,
      within-metric, regime-matched; CROSS-CLOCK magnitude claims
      (gpu_ns vs host_ns) INADMISSIBLE (different spans: kernel only
      vs submit -> completion). Named hazard (design reasoning, NOT a
      measurement): fixed per-dispatch overhead common to both arms
      biases host_ns paired ratios toward 1 -- conservative against
      win claims -- asymmetric arm overhead not assumed away; the
      instrument's stop event records on the host INSIDE the host
      span (named in the instrument). Every magnitude publication
      keeps both clocks' raw per-block times beside the ratios.
  (d) Rule 4 unchanged: this adoption measures nothing and moves
      nothing.
