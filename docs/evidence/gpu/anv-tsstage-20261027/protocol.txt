0. ROW QUESTION (from session 4's named residual, verbatim scope):
   "WHY the small-B bracket undercounts" -- session 4 MEASURED that the
   batch instrument's GPU bracket (ts1..ts2 around B dispatches) is
   31 ticks (FLOOR) at B<=2 at all 18 pipeline-ok cells while the SAME
   runs' host_ns shows 351,956 ns of real work at B=1, and that capture
   turns on between B=2 and B=4 (per-dispatch flat band 3,455..4,370
   ticks for B>=16). NO causal mechanism has been claimed or measured.
   This session does NOT test a causal mechanism. It measures ONE
   narrower, observable question:

     IS THE SMALL-B UNDERCOUNT (AND THE LARGE-B CAPTURE) REMOVABLE BY
     THE PIPELINE-STAGE PLACEMENT OF THE TWO TIMESTAMP ENDPOINTS?

   The instrument brackets its BATCH dispatches between
     ts1: vkCmdWriteTimestamp(..., VK_PIPELINE_STAGE_TOP_OF_PIPE_BIT, .., 0)
     ts2: vkCmdWriteTimestamp(..., VK_PIPELINE_STAGE_BOTTOM_OF_PIPE_BIT, .., 1)
   (the archived session-4 source, lines 275/284). Both endpoints'
   stages are compile-time parameters in this session's derived
   instrument. Whatever the answer, no mechanism is claimed: a stage
   placement that changes the bracket is an OBSERVABLE DEPENDENCE, not
   an explanation.
