PART 2 -- THE DECISION (adopted contract)
=====================================================================

DECIDED: operator-aware selection EXISTS on the CUDA route. The
selection surface gains an operator axis, under this contract:

(1) UNIT + SURFACE. A per-operator record keys :operators #{op}
    (exactly one reduce operator, one of :sum/:max/:min) and applies
    only to KIRs whose :kernel/operator is that operator. The
    existing POOLED records (:operators #{:sum :max :min}) are
    unchanged and keep answering every operator wherever no per-op
    record applies. PRECEDENCE: a per-op record (narrower, fresh-
    session-backed) answers its operator at its W; the pooled layer
    answers everything else. Expressibility is as the cell-unit row
    named it: the KIR carries the reduce operator and select-variant
    already reads KIR facts -- this is a record/query key, not a new
    fact channel.

(2) ENTRY GROUP. (op, W) with :shapes QUALIFYING-ONLY (the pooled
    contract's record shape (3) at its letter, with :operators
    single-op and the group's qualifying shapes listed per (op, W)).
    The record's :device/:channel/:route/:workgroup-size/:evidence
    keys are the pooled shape's, unchanged.

(3) ENTRY RULE (unique-winner + both-clocks + CROSS-RUN INVARIANCE).
    v enters as the group's winner iff in EVERY qualifying reading
    (one reading = one (session, metric) over the group's three pair
    blocks; the statistic is the block's paired-median ratio -- the
    protocol's own statistic, unchanged) v is faster than BOTH
    rivals. Anything else -- a scoped pair among the candidates, a
    cycle, no arm beating every rival, any reading disagreeing --
    REFUSES and keeps the design ladder's answer (or the pooled
    layer's answer) with no record. NAMED AS NEW: at this unit
    rule 2's REGIME machinery has no pool (one block per (op, W,
    shape, pair-type, session) -- there is nothing for rule 1's
    GAP-1.15 clustering to cluster), so REGIME-INVARIANCE is
    REPLACED at this unit by CROSS-RUN INVARIANCE: every qualifying
    reading (every session x both clocks x every qualifying shape)
    must agree one-sided the same way. This is a substitution, not a
    weakening: the invariance demanded spans the axis the pooled
    machine could not see (operators) and the axis it demanded
    (sessions), and it loses the pooled machine's clock-regime
    tolerance -- at this unit a single clock regime's disagreement
    refuses the group.

(4) REPLICATION REQUIREMENT (the pre-registration's teeth). EVERY
    per-op entry -- confirm or flip -- requires at least TWO fresh
    paired-timing sessions run UNDER THIS REGISTRATION on at least
    TWO different UTC days (one fresh session + one sanctioned
    cross-day replication), agreeing on the group's winner in every
    reading of both. The two committed adopted sessions (run 1 /
    run 2, both 2026-10-25) are EXPLORATORY forever: they GENERATED
    the hypotheses below and are same-day besides -- they can satisfy
    no entry, and are flip-note PROSE ONLY (ADR-0373's same-day
    naming rule by citation). Same-day extra sessions corroborate in
    prose only. A flip (a real answer change) is therefore the
    STRONGEST-currency event on this channel: it cannot land before
    the second admissible session's cross-day replication exists.

(5) PRE-REGISTERED HYPOTHESES (registered here, BEFORE any admissible
    session runs; the measurement protocol is fixed here so the
    fresh sessions cannot be shaped after the fact):

    H1 (the standing candidate): at (op :sum, W = 64) on xavier
       sm_72 on the CUDA route, :subgroup-reduce is the unique
       winner -- faster than BOTH :warp-shuffle-reduce and :baseline
       in the paired-median ratio of every qualifying reading (each
       admissible session x both clocks x both shapes n = 1000003
       and 1048576).

    H2 (the contrast): at every other (op, W) group -- :sum at
       W = 32/128/256/512/1024 and :max/:min at all six widths --
       the unique winner is :warp-shuffle-reduce (the design
       answer; these groups would CONFIRM, answer text unchanged).

    FIXED PROTOCOL for the admissible sessions: the ADR-0358
    paired-statistic instrument class
    (scripts/gpu-cuda-reduce-timing.cu on xavier under nvcc 11.4),
    the full grid 2 shapes x 6 widths x 3 ops x 3 pair types = 108
    pair blocks, ONE FRESH PROCESS PER BLOCK, PAIRS=20 interleaved,
    WARMUP=2 per arm, AB/BA alternating, an LCG seed published in
    each session's record, gpu_ns CUDA-event + host_ns
    CLOCK_MONOTONIC per dispatch, untimed verified read-back per
    arm, correctness re-verified inside the measurement, and the
    log published through the committed classifier routes
    (--cuda-log / --cuda-cells). The per-op census route does NOT
    exist yet (see Part 4) -- until the wiring row lands, an
    admissible session's per-op census is derived by the same
    committed-parser route this tick used and recorded as evidence
    text.

    OUTCOME IF A HYPOTHESIS FAILS: a group whose admissible readings
    disagree REFUSES -- no record, no answer change, and the failure
    is recorded at its group. Pre-registration includes the failure
    mode.

(6) SCOPE (contract point 5 at its letter): answer cells of the CUDA
    route ONLY; sign claims stay CHANNEL-SCOPED and DEVICE-SCOPED to
    the tested class (xavier sm_72 -- the uB5G name fragment AND the
    reported compute capability 7.2, both, fail closed, exactly as
    cuda-entry-cell keys them); rung-2 answer cells can never attach
    a per-op record (the tested class reports 7.2 by definition);
    NO workgroup row answers or moves (ADR-0355's channel rule).
    The name-fragment key's coarseness RIDES ALONG unchanged (a
    Jetson Xavier NX descriptor, also sm_72, still reads as the
