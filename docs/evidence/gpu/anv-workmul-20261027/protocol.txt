   groups = ceil(n/W)); an arm (B, M) runs B dispatches of an M-times
   grid inside the bracket = B*M work units total. WORK_MUL=1 is the
   archived behaviour (control rows pin this). Whatever the answer, no
   mechanism is claimed.
