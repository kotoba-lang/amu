## What changed

A public compatible entry clears x5. Private terminal B entry skips exactly that
word only when the caller has completed an original checked access and its actual
outgoing argument descriptor is the unchanged local recorded by the witness.
Both caller and target must satisfy the closed mode2 Vec→Vec contract: one
parameter, at most3 locals/temporaries, zero frame/spills/outgoing calls, no
returning/indirect/unknown calls, allocation, capability or floating operation.
x3 length/x4 item base/x5 positive validated handle are spare under that contract.
Layout checks the actual B opcode and public marker; function addresses, exports
and unproved edges stay public. The contract records XF-AUX1 in MEMORY-MAP/SIR.

Within a function, a positive witness can omit handle equality and descriptor
reload at a later access of the same unchanged local. Its own index bounds check
remains. Branches/labels and unknown effects erase the witness; both explicit
and coalesced writes to that local invalidate it. Admitted integer operations and
original memory-based fuel preserve the descriptor registers. No access or trap
moves ahead of original fuel, and the published five-word fuel sequence remains.

The initial witness is conditional: key0 OR a descriptor matching the entry root.
A cold root access validates at its original location; a positive key may take a
single CBNZ under the proved entry contract. Conditional state cannot cross a
private tail as a completed-access witness. Actual original entry labels erase
this conditional state, so **zero original accesses use that optimization** in
this campaign. Independent cold/private fixtures still exercise it. Do not count
it as a measured benefit.

Read-only observers produce exactly the quiet candidate bytes/offsets. All19
original SIR, frame/local/depth/leaf metadata and fuel match. Machine audit verifies
289 private B targets with positive matching actual argument descriptors,
390 cold-entry functions,119 accesses omitting equality through positive witnesses,
and1,806 original fuel prefixes. These are static counts, not cost shares.

results. Generator, layout and internal ABI source pins remain distinct artifact
